How it works
  1. Scope. Every run starts with Kalshi's open NBA player props (points, rebounds, assists, threes, and the combo stats). If Kalshi has none, nothing else is fetched and no free quota is spent.
  2. Line matching. A Kalshi contract like "25+ points" pays YES if the player scores 25 or more, which is the same bet as sportsbook Over 24.5. A book only counts if it posts both Over and Under at exactly that line. Different lines are listed under "Line mismatches", never stretched to fit.
  3. Removing the vig. For each book: implied% = 100/(odds+100) for plus odds, or |odds|/(|odds|+100) for minus odds. Fair Over = Over implied ÷ (Over implied + Under implied). Consensus = simple average of the books' fair Over. DFS apps and prediction markets are excluded from the consensus.
  4. Kalshi cost. YES buys at the YES ask; NO buys at the NO ask. Taker fee = round up(0.07 × contracts × price × (1 − price)) to the cent, divided by your order size. Cost per contract = ask + fee.
  5. Edge = fair probability of that side − cost per contract, in percentage points. A 60% fair chance bought at 55.7¢ all-in is a 4.3-point edge.
  6. Qualifies only when edge ≥ your minimum, the bid/ask spread on that side ≤ your max, total volume ≥ your minimum, and someone is actually offering contracts at the ask.
  7. Confidence % (Phase 1) = the consensus fair probability of the side you'd buy. Phase 3 adds the context adjustment (recent form, defense vs. position, comps) and shows the exact formula here.
  8. Refresh reloads the latest automated data, then tries to pull live Kalshi prices straight from your browser. Sportsbook odds only update on the scheduled runs (or when you run the workflow by hand), to protect the free quota.
